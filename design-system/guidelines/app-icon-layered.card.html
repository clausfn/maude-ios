How it maps to Icon Composer: each arc is a separate layer with its own translucency and a top-edge specular highlight; the system adds depth shadows between layers, a surface sheen, and concentric corner radii. The shipped artwork stays on-palette — Oxford Navy ground, white mark, the green iris ring as the lit accent — and the system derives Default / Dark / Clear / Tinted automatically. Export layers as PNG/SVG with transparent backgrounds; never bake the shadows into the artwork.
